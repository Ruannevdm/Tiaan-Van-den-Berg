About Tiaan

A Financial Planner Who Puts You First

"To add value and change people's lives through professional advice and holistic financial planning."

Tiaan van den Berg is a dedicated Financial Planner operating under Trident, an affiliate of Liberty — one of South Africa's most established financial services groups. He believes that great financial planning goes beyond numbers: it's about understanding your life, your goals, and building a roadmap that gives you genuine peace of mind.

Whether you're just starting your financial journey, preparing for retirement, or looking to protect what you've built, Tiaan brings clarity, integrity, and personalised guidance to every client relationship.
